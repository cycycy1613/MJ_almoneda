Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before reserving your unit.

How much is the reservation fee? +

Reservation fees are typically affordable and vary per property and unit type. Message us for the exact, current reservation fee for Urban Deca Homes Batangas or our townhouse listings.

How does Pag-IBIG financing work? +

Pag-IBIG Fund offers home loans with low interest rates and long payment terms of up to 30 years. We assist you with requirements, application, and follow-through until loan approval.

Can OFWs qualify to buy a home here? +

Yes. OFWs are strongly encouraged and prioritized by Pag-IBIG financing programs. We regularly assist OFW clients abroad through online consultations, digital document submission, and video-call site tours.

How much are the monthly payments? +

Monthly payments depend on the unit, down payment terms, and loan tenure. We'll prepare a free, personalized computation based on your budget and income so you know exactly what to expect.

Can I schedule a site viewing? +

Yes! We offer both in-person and virtual site viewings. Simply fill out the contact form or message us to book a schedule that works for you.

What documents do I need to prepare? +

Basic requirements include valid IDs, proof of income, and Pag-IBIG membership records. We'll give you a complete, personalized checklist once you begin your application.
